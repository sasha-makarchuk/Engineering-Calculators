Weld Group Analysis Methodology

This calculator implements the standard Elastic Method (Unit Line Method) for weld group statics, consistent with the AISC Steel Construction Manual and AWS D1.1 Structural Welding Code. Welds are treated as line segments with effective throat thickness $t = 0.7071 \cdot h$.

1. Effective Throat & Area

For a standard equal-leg fillet weld with leg size $h$:

$$t = h \cdot \sin(45^\circ) \approx 0.7071 \cdot h, \quad A_w = t \cdot L_{total}$$

2. Centroid of Weld Group Line Pattern

The centroid $(\bar{x}, \bar{y})$ of the multi-segment weld pattern is determined using the first moment of area method:

$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum L_i \cdot x_i}{\sum L_i}, \quad \bar{y} = \frac{\sum L_i \cdot y_i}{\sum L_i}$$

3. Unit vs Total Moments of Inertia

Unit line inertias ($I_{xw}, I_{yw}, J_w$) are calculated per unit throat, then converted to actual section inertias:

$$I_x = t \cdot I_{xw}, \quad I_y = t \cdot I_{yw}, \quad J = t \cdot J_w = I_x + I_y$$

4. Induced Moments & Total Vector Stresses

Loads applied at location $(x_L, y_L, z_L)$ induce additional moments about the weld group centroid:

$$\mathbf{M}_{total} = \mathbf{M}_{applied} + \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{F}$$
$$\tau_{x,k} = \frac{F_x}{A_w} - \frac{M_{z,total} \cdot (y_k - \bar{y})}{J}, \quad \tau_{y,k} = \frac{F_y}{A_w} + \frac{M_{z,total} \cdot (x_k - \bar{x})}{J}$$
$$\tau_{z,k} = \frac{F_z}{A_w} + \frac{M_{x,total} \cdot (y_k - \bar{y})}{I_x} - \frac{M_{y,total} \cdot (x_k - \bar{x})}{I_y}$$
$$\tau_{resultant,k} = \sqrt{\tau_{x,k}^2 + \tau_{y,k}^2 + \tau_{z,k}^2}$$

5. Safety Factor Evaluation

$$\text{Factor of Safety } (n) = \frac{\tau_{allow}}{\max(\tau_{resultant})}$$
